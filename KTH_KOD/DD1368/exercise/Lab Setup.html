Lab Setup

  1. Students are responsible for setting up their lab environment:

    • To setup your lab envoroment read PostgreSQL Installation Guide.pdf

       

    • Students are responsible for adjusting and complementing the data if and where they feel is needed. 
    • To create a local lab environment read Local Lab Setup-1.pdf
  2. This is a PSQL lab environment. No other programming languages, either embedded in the database or external to it, are allowed. 
  3.  Have the terminal ready for the tasks. Make sure you are saving all your insert statements. PostgreSQL uses the “.psql” file format. You can use your preferred text editing software.

    Each student group must book a seat to present his or her lab solutions:

    • To book your seat at a lab session read How to book a lab seat using Canvas instructions.
