Lab Assessment

Lab Assessment 50% (3hp)

  1. The lab assessment is indicated with a Pass or Fail criteria.
  2. There are 4 obligatory lab presentations, all must be passed to gain a final grade in LAB2 on Ladok
  3. The Case Study Case Study 2025.pdf is the main source document to be applied in the course labs.
  4. The labs are accumulative! Make sure to backup all your files
  5. Each lab includes a P+ section, each answered completely and correctly will increase the overall grade by 1. And a total of 4P+ 
    • Only an approved/ passed lab can earn the P+ grade.
    • P+ does not apply for re-exam submissions.
    • P+ tasks focus on the learning goal "present independently acquired necessary knowledge orally and in writing."
  6. Student groups are required to present their (P+) solution during the same scheduled slot booked for the lab presentation. It is recommended that each group add a comment to their booking indicating their intention to present the P+ solution.
  7. Students will work in groups of two not more:
    • Grading for labs is individual. This means both students must always be able to answer all questions on all program code, no matter who wrote it.

    • Take into consideration scheduling when finding a lab partner.

    • The labs are designed and require the efforts of two people working together.
  8.  No submission on Canvas is required.
  9. If you have not received your grade you must inform us before the next lab deadline.

 

LAB GRADING CRITERIA:

Each of the following criteria will be marked with either Achieved, Needs improvements or not achieved.

FX Guidelines:

  • A group will receive Fx if Criteria 1 is not achieved (it is obligatory for passing the assignment, must be Achieved)
  • A group will receive Fx if missing a presentation deadline.
    • Fx, requires resubmitting /completion based on the scheduled date for re-exam. 
    • If the group does not submit Fx in time, the lab is not approved (F)
