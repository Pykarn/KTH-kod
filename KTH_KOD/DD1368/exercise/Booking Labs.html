Booking Labs

Students must book their lab seats via Canvas Calendar no exceptions to the rule.

For instructions regarding using the calendar watch the video on the canvas page How to book a lab seat using Canvas

or read the following link

1. Booking your seat:

  • Labs will be presented on Campus, read the course schedule for details. 
  • Please respect your group categories (Even and Odd) when booking a seat.
  • You will work in teams of two students, so both you and your friend must book the session you prefer.
  • No booking means no seat, and therefore no lab. 
  • You book a seat for each lab separately: one booking for lab1, another one for lab2, and so on.
  • You can not present several labs within the same booking.

2. Lab Guidelines:

  • Students can only attend/book a seat to the group and session they are assigned to.
    • Students can be asked to leave if signing up for the wrong session
  • Booking for labs will close at 13.00, one working day before the lab session, and so on.
  • After the booking has closed, you cannot change your booking. If you don't show up, you lose your reserved seat.
  • If you have a seat,  on the day of the booking please use https://queue.eecs.kth.se/ to be assigned to a TA.
    • If you are 15 minutes late, or more, you lose your seat.
  • If you have not received your grade you must inform us before the next deadline.
