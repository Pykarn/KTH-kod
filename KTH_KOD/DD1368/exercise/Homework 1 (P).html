HOMEWORK 1 (Overview)

To quickly get an overview of a database design, an ER diagram can be used to show other database designers how a database can look like. 

Important requirements and information:

  • A maximum of 8 pages including the ER and UML diagrams.
  • Applications such as https://app.diagrams.net/ are recommended although a clear hand drawn diagram is also acceptable. Please refrain from using dark mode in diagrams.
  • Note that links to diagrams are not allowed.
