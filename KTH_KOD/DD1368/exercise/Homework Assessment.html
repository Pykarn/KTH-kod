Homework Assessment

Homework Assessment 50% (3hp)

  1. The homework assessment is indicated with a Pass or Fail criteria.
  2. There are 4 obligatory homework's, all must be passed to gain a final grade in HEM1 on Ladok. They can be found under Assignments
  3. The Case Study is the main source document to be applied in the course exercises including homework’s.
  4. The homework's are accumulative! Make sure they are contestant and coherent.
  5. Each homework includes P+ section, if answered complete and correctly it will increase the overall grade by 1. 
    • Only an approved/ passed homework can earn the P+ grade.
    • P+ does not apply for re-exam submissions.
    • Students can earn a maximum of 1P+ credits per homework. And a total of 4P+
    • P+ tasks focus on the learning goal "present independently acquired necessary knowledge orally and in writing."
  6. Students will work in groups of two not more, to do so go to People

    • The homework is designed and requires the efforts of two people working together

  7. Your report must be handed in via Canvas only, and must take into consideration the following quality guidelines:
    • The entire report must be in one file (Pdf, Doc)
    • No external links are allowed in the document.
    • No screenshots/screen captures.
    • Both student names should be included in the document.
  8. If you have not received your grade you must inform us before the next homework deadline.
  9. Students are not allowed to copy, cut, or paste any text into your report that is not produced by you. The only exception is if you quote text properly, and give a citation to the original source. 

Fx Guidelines:

  1. A group will receive Fx, if criteria 1 is not achieved (obligatory for passing the assignment must be an Achieved criteria)
  2. A group will receive Fx, if missing a submission deadline.
    • Fx, requires resubmitting /completion based on the scheduled date for re-exam. 
    • All homework’s re-exam is week 2 (2026)
    • For homework re-exam, all homework’s on canvas will reopen at the end of the course and their you resubmit.
    • If the group does not submit Fx in time, homework is not approved (F) 
  3. All re-submitted homework’s will then be graded after the deadline.
