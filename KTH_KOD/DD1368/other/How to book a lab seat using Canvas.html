How to book a lab seat using Canvas

Booking your seat:

Students must book their seats via Canvas Calendar. For instructions regarding using the calendar read the following link

or watch this short video (note video has no audio)

  • Students can only attend/book a seat to the group and session they are assigned to.
    • Student can be asked to leave if signing up for the wrong session
    • Book early, so that you get a seat at the session you prefer
    • The objective of the groups is to organize the sessions only, and create an effective environment for all
    • If you are 15 minutes late, or more, you lose your seat.
    • Please respect your group number when booking a seat
