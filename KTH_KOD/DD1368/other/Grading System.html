Grading System

Grading Details:

The course consists of two main assessments, this applies to all students, even re-registered students.

      1. Students must complete and pass both HEM1 and LAB2 to gain a final grade.

        • Both have strict deadlines. Check the  Syllabus for details.

        • All homework's must be completed and passed to earn a final grade under HEM1
        • All labs must be completed and passed to earn a final grade under LAB2
      2. Students cannot carry partial lab and/or homework grades from different years/attempts.
      3. All homework's and labs are based on a case study designed specifically for the course DD1368 and is the main source document to be applied in the course Case Study 2026.pdf
        • Note that all assignments include a P+ section.
        • Students will work in teams of two for both homework's/labs, to do so go to People
        • If you are missing a grade for homework's/labs you must inform us in good time.
      4. If you have not passed a homework and/or lab by that time, then you will have to repeat all Assessments for this course in the following year.
      5. For re-registered students only, with no grade/did not pass on TEN1
        To finish the course and earn a finalized course grade, you will need to register for the course HT24-25 and do all homework’s (HEM1) to earn a grade corresponding to the old TEN1 course written exam.         Note homework’s will be with deadlines during the entire course

Course Grading System:

Students must pass both HEM1 (50%) and LAB2 (50%) to earn a final course grade.

Check the Syllabusfor details.

Reporting Grades: 

We will only report grades to LADOK via Canvas in batches. This means that all students that have finished all labs and the homework's at the end of the course, grades will be reported at the same time at the end of the course.
