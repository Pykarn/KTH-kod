Communication Guidelines & support contacts

Communication guidelines for all students :

Due to the mass amount of daily emails, I would like to recommend some general guidelines for sending an email. The objective is that your email does not get lost/overseen or neglected.

  1. All students must follow the Code of Conduct
  2. Emails regarding Registration or access rights, IT, etc… Should be sent to service@eecs.kth.se  or www.kth.se/eecs/studentsupport (Note Unfortunately I cannot help with these issues) 
  3. Students with special educational needs can contact https://www.kth.se/student/stod/studier/funktionsnedsattning directly to get the support they need.

When sending a direct email, please consider the following:

Try to ensure that your email is relevant to the course, and:

  1. Use your KTH email account.
  2. Take into consideration that replying to emails is during official working hours. Replying can take 3-4 working days. 
  3. The email subject must include the course code, in this case DD1368 ONLY.
  4. Identify yourself clearly. (ex: Full name included in the email body).
  5. Keep it brief; not more than one short paragraph.
  6. Keep the message focused.
  7. Avoid attachments.
