General Overview

The overall aim of the course is to equip you with the skills to model and implement database solutions. All course material will be given in English for course syllabubs read 

https://www.kth.se/student/kurser/kurs/DD1368/?l=sv

 

This course is loosely based on CS145: Introduction to Databases given at Stanford University and uses the textbook:

H. Garcia-Molina, J. D. Ullman and J. Widom, Database Systems: The Complete Book (Second Edition), Pearson. (2009 or 2013 or 2014) ISBN (978-0131873254, 978-0-13-135428 or 978-1-292-02447-9)

Student who feel they need recording of lectures, I would recommend watching Databases - Jennifer Widom - Stanford Stanford Dbclass  who is the one of the book authors we are following in the course. Ex:

01-01-introduction.mp4

 

Course Registration

You must register for the course using KTH's web system. If you are re-registering for the course, you should go to the following link and send in a registration request. If you have any questions about the course registration, please go to the student support/service center.

    • Emails regarding Registration or access rights, IT, etc… Should be sent to service@eecs.kth.se  or www.kth.se/eecs/studentsupport (Note Unfortunately I cannot help with these issues) 
    • Students with special educational needs can contact https://www.kth.se/student/stod/studier/funktionsnedsattning  directly to get the support they need.
    • Students will need to choose early in the course to join groups, since all assessments have specific hand-in dates and deadlines including labs, please check the course schedule and course plan for details.
    • The objective of the groups to create effective time and schedule for all students to present their lab assignments on time.
    • In general, please try to attend all the lectures and support sessions.
