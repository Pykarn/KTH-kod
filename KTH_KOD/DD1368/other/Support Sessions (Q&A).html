Support Sessions (Q&A)

To support all students during their studies the course includes several Q&A support sessions.

Main Objective of the sessions:

  • The Q&A  sessions are not an assessment session but a support session.
  • Students can come in to brainstorm their thoughts with the TA team
  • These sessions are not obligatory to attend.

  • Each session is pre-scheduled (Check the schedule for location/room details)

    The objective of the booking is to ensure all groups receive enough time and don't waste time waiting for a TA to become available.

    • Students must book their Q&A seats via Canvas Calendar. 
    • Booking closes one days before the session
  • Students can get support on both homework and labs during these sessions. The benefit from any help you get is much greater if you have put in an honest effort first to figure it out.
  • All students must follow the Code of Conduct  regulations
